About Alix

A Young Rider With A Bigger Purpose

Alix Hernandez is a young bull rider building his future one ride at a time. His journey is driven by determination, discipline, courage and a passion for rodeo.

Every competition brings another opportunity to learn, improve and move closer to his goal of competing at the professional level.

For Alix, bull riding is about more than eight seconds. It is about building character, representing the people who support him and creating a future through hard work.
