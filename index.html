About Alix

A Young Rider With a Bigger Purpose

Alix Hernandez is a 17-year-old bull rider from Ault, Colorado, competing under the banner of Spur Ride Rodeo Company.

In just two years, Alix has already earned multiple Reserve Champion titles — a reflection of his raw talent, relentless dedication, and true western grit.

Alix is seeking sponsorship support to reach the next level of competition and continue chasing his goals in the rodeo world.
