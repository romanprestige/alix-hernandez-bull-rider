To My Sponsors, Family & Supporters

First and foremost, I want to say thank you. Whether you're a business considering sponsoring me, someone who has already stepped forward to support my journey, or family and friends who have stood beside me since I was just a little kid in Little Britches, I am truly grateful.

Bull riding isn't just about climbing on a bull and holding on for eight seconds. It's about hard work, courage, determination, and the people who believe in you even when the road gets tough.

Every sponsorship, big or small, means more to me than words can explain. When your company's name or brand is sewn onto my riding gear, I want you to know that I wear it with pride. You're not just helping me compete. You're becoming part of my journey.

I want my sponsors to feel connected to what they're supporting. I'll share upcoming rodeo schedules, event updates, and news about my progress.

I also want every sponsor to have the opportunity to meet and talk with me personally. Whether that's stopping by your business, attending a company picnic, arranging a meet-and-greet, connecting through a video call, sending emails, or simply making a quick phone call, I want to build genuine relationships.

My sponsors will also be invited as special guests to rodeo events. Whenever possible, I'd love to welcome you to an event near your business or have you join me at a rodeo farther away. Event arrangements and travel would be coordinated individually.

To my family and loved ones, thank you for being there through the early mornings, long drives, practices, victories, and tough rides. You helped make me the person I am today.

I know I wouldn't be where I am without the people standing behind me. I hope to make every one of you proud, both inside and outside the arena.

Thank you for believing in me and for being part of something bigger than just one ride.

With sincere gratitude,
Alix Hernandez
